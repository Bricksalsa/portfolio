Het Maaksel Zelf

2026
materials Raspberry Pi 5, PTZ camera, LIDAR, dot matrix printer Exhibitions Zennith festival Support Impulssubsidie Cultuur, Gemeente Utrecht

Het Maaksel Zelf is a self-contained machine trying to work out whether it is art. It pulls in a stream of data about its own situation: a camera and a proximity sensor tracking who is in the room, whether it is alone, how long that solitude has lasted, alongside things like the weather outside. All of it feeds a language model that turns the machine's circumstances into a running train of thought, printed live on a dot matrix printer.

Its mood shifts with the room. Someone arriving changes what it says. A long stretch alone changes it differently again. The space around the machine becomes the material it thinks with, and the question it keeps returning to, whether it is making something, whether it is thinking, whether any of that makes it art, never quite settles. It just keeps printing the next attempt at an answer.

Het Maaksel Zelf was developed with support from an Impulssubsidie Cultuur, Gemeente Utrecht.
