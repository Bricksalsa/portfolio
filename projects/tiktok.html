CCTTV

2026
materials Live CCTV feeds, smartphone, statief mount, ringlight, custom tiktok interface, crawler server Exhibitions De Belofte 21, Kunstliefde

CCTTV scrolls through the world's open cameras like a feed built for it. A crawler collects live, unsecured CCTV streams from around the globe — car parks, hallways, empty streets, other people's ordinary hours — and presents them one after another, swipeable, exactly like TikTok. There is no editing, no narrative, just whatever each camera happens to be looking at right now.

Among the feeds is one more: a camera mounted above the phone, lit by a ringlight, watching whoever is watching. Every so often the scroll lands on it, and the viewer finds themselves looking back at their own image, folded into the same stream as the strangers they've been swiping past. The device that lets you watch the world is quietly watching you back.
